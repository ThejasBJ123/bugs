Product Line #2 • Poultry Nutrition & Hatchery Feeds

Poultry Feed Bags & Sacks

High-gloss BOPP reverse printed woven bags crafted to retain moisture levels, prevent nutrient degradation, and give feed brands unmatched shelf distinction in commercial markets.

Aroma Retention & High Impact Retail Presentation

Broiler and layer feed formulations contain volatile aroma compounds and sensitive proteins. Rayashree Weaving's multi-layer BOPP sandwich laminated bags seal in freshness while providing crystal-clear 8-color rotogravure printing that elevates brand trust among poultry farmers.

Distinct Engineering Features:

  • 8-Color High-Gloss BOPP: Reverse photographic printing protects artwork from scuffing, water, and direct sunlight.
  • Precision Side Gusseting: Creates square edges for clean vertical retail shelf standing and barcode scanning.
  • Ergonomic Carry Handles: D-cut or top loop handle options for convenient 25kg retail handling.
  • 100% Virgin Polymer: Odorless, non-toxic, and approved for animal feed direct contact.
