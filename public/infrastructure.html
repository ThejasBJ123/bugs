World-Class Manufacturing Infrastructure

Equipped with European Starlinger and Lohia high-speed circular weaving looms, micro-extrusion lines, and computerized conversion units delivering 5,000,000+ sacks monthly.

Step 1: Polymer Extrusion

High-Tenacity Tape Extrusion Lines

Using 100% prime virgin PP/HDPE polymer granules and color/UV masterbatches, our extrusion lines melt, cast, orient, and wind micro-uniform flat tapes with minimum denier deviation and supreme tensile tenacity.

  • • Capacity: 350 MT / Month Polymer Throughput
  • • Denier Range: 600 Denier to 2200 Denier
  • • Online thickness gauging and auto-tension winders
Step 2: Circular & Flat Looms

48+ High-Speed Circular Weaving Looms

Our plant houses 4-shuttle, 6-shuttle, and 8-shuttle circular weaving looms operating 24/7. Capable of producing tubular woven rolls from 12 inches up to 100 inches layflat width, with anti-skid diamond textures and colored identification weaves.

  • • Weave Density: 8x8 to 14x14 mesh counts per inch
  • • Zero-oil contamination magnetic shuttles
  • • Automatic weft break optical sensors with continuous electronic monitoring
Step 3: High-Definition In-Line Printing

BCS 6 Colour Online Flexo Printing Machine

Integrated directly with our high-speed conversion flow, the BCS 6 Colour Online Flexo Printing Machine provides synchronized, high-speed multi-color printing on tubular woven fabric rolls. Delivers razor-sharp brand graphics, micro-text, QR codes, and multi-color agricultural/industrial branding with zero smudging.

  • • Color Configuration: 6-Colour Precision Flexo (Front & Back 6+0 / 4+2 setup)
  • • Printing Speed: Up to 120 – 150 Meters / Minute with hot-air drying chamber
  • • Micro-Registration: Electronic mark sensors ensuring pinpoint print accuracy
  • • Eco-Friendly Inks: Non-toxic, food-grade compliant inks for feed & food packaging
Step 4: Automated Bag Conversion

BCS Cutting Machine (Lohia Bag Conversion System)

Our Lohia BCS Cutting Machine delivers automated high-speed bag conversion from woven rolls. It integrates precision heat cutting (anti-fray seal), synchronized mouth opening, single or double bottom folding, and multi-thread heavy chain stitching in one seamless automated pass.

  • • Production Output: 35 to 45 finished bags per minute continuous speed
  • • Cutting Dimensions: Bag length 500 mm – 1400 mm • Bag width 300 mm – 850 mm
  • • Optical Detection: Electronic eye mark sensor for printed fabric cutting precision
  • • Anti-Fraying Technology: Controlled temperature hot knife cut with automated batch stacker
Step 5: BOPP Lamination & AD*STAR Converting

Rotogravure Lamination & AD*STAR Valve Finishing

Equipped with multi-color roll-to-roll rotogravure printing, high-speed corona surface treaters, extrusion lamination machines, and automated hot-air AD*STAR block bottom valve bag converting lines.

  • • Full photographic CMYK + spot color high-gloss BOPP lamination
  • • Automated hot-air AD*STAR block bottom converting (no glue/adhesive)
  • • Ultrasonic hem cutting and heavy safety-lock chain stitching
