World-Class Manufacturing Infrastructure

Equipped with European Starlinger and Lohia high-speed circular weaving looms, micro-extrusion lines, and computerized conversion units delivering 5,000,000+ sacks monthly.

Step 1: Polymer Extrusion

High-Tenacity Tape Extrusion Lines

Using 100% prime virgin PP/HDPE polymer granules and color/UV masterbatches, our extrusion lines melt, cast, orient, and wind micro-uniform flat tapes with minimum denier deviation and supreme tensile tenacity.

  • • Capacity: 350 MT / Month Polymer Throughput
  • • Denier Range: 600 Denier to 2200 Denier
  • • Online thickness gauging and auto-tension winders
Step 2: Circular & Flat Looms

48+ High-Speed Circular Weaving Looms

Our plant houses 4-shuttle, 6-shuttle, and 8-shuttle circular weaving looms operating 24/7. Capable of producing tubular woven rolls from 12 inches up to 100 inches layflat width, with anti-skid diamond textures and colored identification weaves.

  • • Weave Density: 8x8 to 14x14 mesh counts per inch
  • • Zero-oil contamination magnetic shuttles
  • • Automatic weft break optical sensors
Step 3: Printing & Conversion

8-Color BOPP Printing & Automated Converting

Equipped with multi-color roll-to-roll rotogravure printing, high-speed corona surface treaters, extrusion lamination machines, and automated heat-cut, sewing, and valve bag welding lines.

  • • Full photographic CMYK + spot color printing
  • • Automated hot-air AD*STAR block bottom converting
  • • Ultrasonic hem cutting and heavy chain stitching
Quality Assurance Lab

Rigorous Batch Testing Protocol
