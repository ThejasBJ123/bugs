About Rayashree Weaving Pvt. Ltd.

Pioneering precision polymer extrusion, high-speed circular weaving, and sustainable packaging solutions from Bangalore to clients nationwide and abroad.

Our Corporate Heritage

Commitment to Zero-Defect Industrial Packaging

Rayashree Weaving Pvt. Ltd. was founded with a clear objective: to manufacture durable, precisely-calibrated, and aesthetically flawless woven packaging for mission-critical industries.

From our advanced industrial plant located in Bangalore North, we operate high-tenacity PP/HDPE tape extrusion lines, automated circular weaving looms, 8-color high-definition printing units, and computerized bag converting lines.

Quality First
