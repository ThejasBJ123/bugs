Product Line #7 • Industrial Heavy Bulk Packaging

Industrial FIBC Bags, HDPE Sacks & Liners

Heavy-duty 500kg to 2,500kg Safe Working Load FIBC bulk bags, standard HDPE/PP sacks, form-fit PE liners, and 20ft/40ft shipping container bulk liners engineered with 5:1 and 6:1 safety factors.

Heavy-Duty Industrial Strength & Bulk Efficiency

Moving bulk powders, grains, minerals, and chemicals requires robust packaging that guarantees zero rupture under heavy crane hoists or sea freight turbulence. Rayashree Weaving manufactures certified U-Panel, 4-Panel, Circular, and Baffle (Q-Bag) FIBCs with custom discharge spouts, heavy lifting loops, and internal barrier liners.

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • Certified Safety Working Load: 500 kg, 1000 kg (1 Ton), 1500 kg, and 2000 kg (2 Ton) ratings with 5:1 (Single trip) or 6:1 (Multi-trip) safety factors.
  • Heavy Duty Lifting Loops: 4 Corner Loops, Cross Corner Loops, or Stevedore Straps made with high-tenacity woven webbing.
  • Internal PE Barrier Liners: Form-fit, glued, or tabbed LDPE/LLDPE liners to eliminate contamination, moisture ingress, and powder sifting.
  • Shipping Container Liners: Dry bulk liner bags for 20-foot and 40-foot sea freight shipping containers.
