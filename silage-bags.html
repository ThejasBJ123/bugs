Product Line #4 • Agricultural Fodder Preservation

Agricultural Silage & Storage Tube Bags

Co-extruded multi-layer UV stabilized silage bags and continuous silo tubes engineered to provide strict anaerobic conditions for preserving nutrient-rich corn, sorghum, and forage grasses.

Maximum Nutritional Value with Zero Dry Matter Loss

Silage preservation requires complete exclusion of oxygen to promote lactic acid fermentation. Our dual-tone white/black barrier technology reflects intense sunlight while completely blocking UV rays, keeping internal silage cool and nutrient-packed for up to 24 months in open weather.

Key Agricultural Advantages:

  • White/Black Dual Thermal Layer: 90% Solar reflectance on outside; 100% light-blocking black barrier on inside.
  • Tear & Puncture Resistance: Fortified with virgin octene/hexene metallocene polymers to resist stalks and rough field conditions.
  • Both Hand-Bags & Continuous Silo Tubes: Available in 50kg - 80kg individual farm sacks or large 9ft/10ft x 300ft machine-filled silo tubes.
  • 24-Month UV Guarantee: Rigorously stabilized for intense Indian tropical sunlight and monsoon downpours.
