Product Line #5 • 100% Eco & Biodegradable Packaging

Eco-Friendly Jute & Burlap Sacks

Sustainable, 100% biodegradable golden natural jute sacks and Hessian cloth engineered with Hydro-Carbon Free (HCF) treatment for international agricultural commodities.

Natural Breathability Meets International Food Safety

Grains, raw coffee beans, and organic produce require breathable natural packaging that absorbs ambient sweat without fostering fungal mildew. Our food-grade hydrocarbon-free jute bags comply strictly with IJO (International Jute Organization) standards for coffee, cocoa, and spice exports.

Key Jute Specifications:

  • Hydro-Carbon Free (HCF): Vegetable oil processed to guarantee zero taint or chemical contamination in specialty food commodities.
  • High Friction Stacking: Natural coarse fiber enables warehouse stacking over 20 bags high without slippage.
  • 100% Renewable & Biodegradable: Decomposes naturally into soil enriched organic matter within months.
  • Distinctive Striping: Standard 1-stripe, 2-stripe, or 3-stripe green/blue identification selvage.
