Product Line #1 • Livestock & Dairy Feeds

Cattle Feed Woven Sacks & Bags

Engineered for high-volume animal feed mills and dairy cooperatives, our PP woven cattle feed sacks deliver uncompromising drop strength, anti-slip warehouse stacking, and high-definition BOPP brand graphics.

Engineered For Rough Handling & Nutrition Preservation

Animal feeds require packaging that resists moisture absorption, preserves essential vitamins, and prevents insect infiltration while tolerating rigorous transport on rural supply routes. Rayashree Weaving uses high-denier circular woven polypropylene fabrics reinforced with micro-embossed anti-skid weaves.

Key Advantages:

  • Zero Burst Risk: Tested up to 2.5 meters drop tests with 50kg contents on concrete.
  • Anti-Slip Diamond Pattern: Allows stacking up to 25 bags high without sliding in warehouses.
  • Full Multi-Color BOPP Printing: High-gloss or matte reverse gravure print for premium brand shelf appeal.
  • Micro-Perforation or PE Liner: Breathable weave for dry mash or sealed liner for high-molasses feed pellets.

Technical Specifications:

Standard Capacities 25 kg, 50 kg, 75 kg (Custom sizes available)
Fabric GSM Range 65 GSM to 120 GSM
Material Composition 100% Virgin Polypropylene (PP) Polymer
Dimensions (Common) 20x36", 22x38", 24x40", 24x42" (Inches)
Denier 700D to 1200D High Tensile Tape
Mesh Count 10x10, 11x11, 12x12 per square inch
Lamination BOPP Sandwich Lamination or PP Extrusion Coating
Bottom Stitching Heavy-duty Single / Double Fold with Chain Stitch
